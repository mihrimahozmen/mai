        Engineering Economy

        A semester-long "SmartCampus Café" investment project where student teams make AI-assisted decisions under uncertainty. A "Verified by Human" protocol, interaction logs, and a three-level engagement rubric train students to check and correct AI outputs instead of accepting them.

        AI Stakeholder Simulation

        Project Management Planning & Control

        Generative-AI role-play in which students negotiate with simulated stakeholders, such as frustrated clients and resistant team members, to build conflict resolution, ethical judgment, and leadership. Includes a shareable prompt library for college-wide use.
